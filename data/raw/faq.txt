## Shipping Information

### What Shipping Methods Are Available?

We offer various shipping methods to accommodate different needs. For domestic shipments within the UAE and occasionally to Oman, KSA, and Bahrain, we rely on road transport. International shipments are primarily handled through air for timely delivery and efficiency. Additionally, for bulk or specific requirements, sea is available. Our diverse shipping options ensure flexibility and reliability, catering to both local and global destinations.

### How Long Will it Take To Get My Package?

The delivery time for your package varies depending on the destination. Typically, international shipments take between 6 to 8 days for completion under normal circumstances. Domestic deliveries are fulfilled within a shorter timeframe, usually ranging from 1 to 2 days. However, factors like customs clearance and unforeseen delays may affect the delivery schedule. Rest assured, we strive to expedite the process and ensure timely delivery of your package, keeping you informed throughout the shipping journey.
