# Citizen CX-02 Technical Datasheet

## Specifications and Print Method
- Model Name: Citizen CX-02
- Printing Technology: Dye-sublimation thermal transfer with ribbon rewind technology
- Resolution: 300 x 300 DPI (High Speed Mode) and 300 x 600 DPI (High Resolution Mode)
- Colors: 3 Colours (Yellow, Magenta, Cyan) plus Protective Laminate Overcoat
- Gradation: 256 levels per colour (16.7 million colours total)
- Image Buffer: 64 MB internal high-speed memory buffer

## Print Sizes and Capacity
- 4 x 6 inches (102 x 152 mm): 400 prints per roll, 800 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS46-2PC)
- 5 x 7 inches (127 x 178 mm): 230 prints per roll, 460 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS57-2PC)
- 6 x 8 inches (152 x 203 mm): 200 prints per roll, 400 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS68-2PC)
- 6 x 9 inches (152 x 229 mm): Supported via specialized 6x9 media
- 2 x 6 inches (51 x 152 mm): Photo booth strips (driver-controlled dual cut feature enabled on 4x6 media or 6x8 media)

## Print Speeds
- 4 x 6 inch: 8.4 seconds in High Speed mode; 9.8 seconds in High Quality mode
- 5 x 7 inch: 14.2 seconds
- 6 x 8 inch: 15.6 seconds
- 6 x 9 inch: 20.8 seconds

## Finishes and Surface Control
- Surface Finishes: Glossy, Matte, and Fine Matte (Luster) finishes available
- Finish Selection: Managed digitally through the printer driver thermal overcoat setting without needing to change paper or ribbon rolls
- Protective Overcoat: Water-resistant, UV-resistant, and fingerprint-resistant lamination layer applied automatically during the 4th pass

## Ribbon Rewind Technology
- Ribbon-Save Feature: When printing odd numbers of 4x6" photos or half-format prints on 6x8" media, the printer automatically rewinds the ribbon, ensuring zero ribbon waste.
- Cost Efficiency: Maximizes yield and eliminates wasted ribbon panels.

## Physical Dimensions and Weight
- Width: 275 mm (10.8 inches)
- Depth: 366 mm (14.4 inches)
- Height: 170 mm (6.7 inches)
- Net Weight: 12.0 kg (26.5 lbs) excluding paper and ribbon
- Gross Packaged Weight: 13.5 kg (29.8 lbs)
- Footprint: 33% smaller and 14% lighter than preceding models (such as CX-01)

## Connectivity and Operating Systems
- Interface: High-speed USB 2.0 (Type-B connector)
- Supported Operating Systems: Windows 7, Windows 8, Windows 10, Windows 11 (32-bit and 64-bit); macOS 10.12 Sierra up to macOS 14 Sonoma; Linux CUPS drivers available
- Power Supply: AC 100V - 240V, 50/60 Hz automatic switching internal power supply
- Power Consumption: Standby power consumption under 0.5W (Sleep Mode complies with energy-saving directives)

## Paper and Ribbon Loading
- Mechanism: Front-loading drop-in paper and ribbon system
- Ease of Use: Designed for quick media changes in cramped photo booths or dark event environments within 60 seconds
- Media Tray: Removable scrap bin captures paper trimmings to keep the work area clean
