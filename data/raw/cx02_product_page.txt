# Citizen CX-02 Product Overview and Applications

## Product Overview
The Citizen CX-02 represents the benchmark in portable, professional dye-sublimation photo printing. Engineered by Citizen Systems Japan, this compact powerhouse delivers laboratory-grade, continuous-tone photo prints in seconds. Known for unmatched durability and reliability in commercial environments, the CX-02 is the preferred printer worldwide for event photographers, photo booth builders, amusement parks, school portraits, and instant retail photo kiosks.

## Target Applications
- Photo Booths: Its ultra-compact chassis (only 170 mm high and 275 mm wide) fits effortlessly inside slim photobooth enclosures, retro wooden booths, or modern mirror booths.
- Event Photography & Weddings: Fast 8.4-second print times keep guests moving and queues short. The optional padded carry bag protects the unit during transit.
- Retail Kiosks & Instant Printing: Front loading paper and ribbon drop-in mechanisms allow non-technical store clerks to reload consumables in seconds.
- ID & Passport Photos: Precision 300x600 dpi resolution produces razor-sharp biometric portraits and crisp detail.

## Key Advantages Over Traditional Printing
- Zero Smudging: Unlike inkjet printers, dye-sub prints emerge completely dry, coated with a durable protective layer that resists water, UV light, and fingerprints immediately upon exit.
- No Ink Cartridges to Clog: Thermal dye-sublimation ribbons never dry out or clog printheads, even after sitting idle for weeks.
- Multiple Finishes from a Single Roll: Switch between glossy, matte, and fine matte finishes directly from the print driver software without replacing paper or changing rolls.
- Energy Efficient Sleep Mode: Lowers power consumption by up to 50% compared to previous generations, consuming less than 0.5W in standby.
