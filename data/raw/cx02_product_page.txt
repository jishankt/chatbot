____ What is the purpose of the ribbon rewind feature in the Citizen CX02 printer?

The ribbon rewind feature helps to use less paper and ribbon. It lets you roll up the ribbon in the printer when you’re done printing, instead of throwing it away. This can help preserve ribbon and paper while also reducing the amount of waste generated by the printer.

____ What type of media does the Citizen CX-02 use?

The CX-02 uses special dye sublimation media, which includes paper and ink ribbon. The printer heats the ink ribbon, causing the dye to vaporize and then bond with the paper’s surface, resulting in high-quality photo prints.
