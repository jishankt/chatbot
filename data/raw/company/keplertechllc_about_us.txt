# About Us

Kepler Tech LLC is a reputable trading company based in the United Arab Emirates, specializing in providing a comprehensive range of IT-related products, technologies, and solutions to various industries. With a strong presence in the UAE and the Middle East region, we have established ourselves as a reliable and trustworthy supplier and partner.

At Kepler Tech LLC, we are committed to providing exceptional products, cutting-edge technologies, and innovative solutions that empower our clients to achieve their business objectives and stay ahead in today’s dynamic market. With our strong partnerships and expertise in IT-related products, we strive to deliver outstanding customer service and build long-lasting relationships with our valued clients across the UAE and the Middle East.

## Products & Services Offered

#### Printing Equipment, Technologies & Solutions for Photography, Graphics & Fine Arts Industries:

We offer a wide range of printing equipment, technologies, and solutions tailored to the specific needs of the photography, graphics, and fine arts industries. Our product portfolio includes commercial photo printers, large format photo printers, workforce printers, as well as a selection of media and consumables required for high-quality printing.

##### Adobe Learning Management Systems for all types of Organizations & Industries:

As an authorized provider, we offer Adobe Learning Management Systems (LMS) that cater to the diverse training needs of organizations across various industries. Our Adobe LMS solutions enable efficient and effective training and development programs, allowing businesses to enhance their workforce’s skills and knowledge.

#### Adobe Workflow Automation & Digital Transformation Solutions for all types of Organizations & Industries:

We provide cutting-edge Adobe workflow automation and digital transformation solutions designed to streamline business processes and optimize efficiency. Our solutions leverage Adobe’s industry-leading technologies to automate workflows, improve collaboration, and facilitate digital transformation initiatives for organizations across different sectors.

## Brands We Represent

We take pride in being an official partner for renowned brands in the printing and related industries, ensuring that our clients have access to top-quality products and services. Some of the brands we represent include:

Photography, Graphics, Fine Arts & Other Industries:

We offer a range of [Epson products](https://www.keplertechllc.com/product-category/printers-ink/printers/?filter_brands=epson), including commercial photo printers, large format photo printers, workforce printers, as well as media and consumables required for printing applications in the photography, graphics, and fine arts industries.

As a partner of Mirage (Dinax), we offer RIP software solutions that provide print workflow control, ensuring efficient and accurate printing processes.

We provide high-quality photo papers and canvas from [KOREJET](https://www.keplertechllc.com/product-category/inkjet-media/?filter_brands=korejet), meeting the demanding requirements of professional printing applications.
