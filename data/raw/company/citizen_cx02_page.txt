# CX-02

## A new level of quality and portability

When quality and portability are critical requirements for your photo printer, look no further than the Citizen CX-02. The CX-02 joins Citizen’s highly regarded CX printer range, offering new levels of quality, portability and compactness.

The CX-02 is 2kg lighter than the CX model and 15% smaller. Even so, it can still produce a lab quality photo print in just 8 seconds. Then there is the new ribbon rewind function. No more wasted ribbon and paper when printing single 6 x 4 inch photos using 6 x 8 inch media. These features combine to make the CX-02 Compact the perfect printer for event photographers, photo party booths and retail kiosks.
