# Citizen CX-02 Troubleshooting, Maintenance & Support Guide

## 1. Print Quality Issues (White Lines & Streaks)
- **Symptom**: Horizontal or vertical white lines, missing print bands, or streakiness across printed photos.
- **Cause**: Dust, ink residue, or thermal debris accumulated on the thermal printhead.
- **Official Kepler Recommendation**:
  - Kepler Tech strongly advises cleaning the thermal printhead each time a new roll of media is installed.
  - Use the official **Citizen Thermal Head Cleaning Pen** (SKU: `CITIZEN-PEN`, internal ID: `CITIZEN-PEN`, Price: 190.00 AED).
  - Gently swipe the pen tip across the thermal printhead elements while the printer is powered off.
  - If white lines persist after cleaning, or if the printhead has sustained a physical scratch, contact Kepler Tech technical support for hardware servicing or replacement.

## 2. Paper Jamming & Strange Noises
- **Paper Jamming**:
  - Ensure the paper roll flanges (blue and white) are correctly seated into their color-coded holders in the scrap box area.
  - Ensure the paper roll edge is cut clean and straight before feeding through the rollers.
  - Advance the paper until the printer beeps and the green LED illuminates.
  - If repeated paper jams occur, do not force the paper path. Hand off to Kepler technical support.
- **Strange Grinding or Clicking Noises**:
  - Check that the scrap box is emptied regularly (empty every roll change).
  - Ensure the ribbon spool gears are fully clicked into position and ribbon slack has been wound taut.
  - If abnormal mechanical noise persists, hand off to Kepler technical service team immediately without self-disassembly.

## 3. Power Issues & Failures
- **Printer Won't Turn On**:
  - Verify that the power switch on the rear panel is in the ON position and the AC power cord is firmly plugged into a grounded wall outlet.
  - If the unit has no power, verify order details and purchase date.
  - Hand off to Kepler Tech customer support with order details for power supply unit (PSU) testing. Do not make unauthorized warranty claims.

## 4. Drivers & Software
- **Official Driver Downloads**:
  - Official Citizen printer drivers for Windows (Windows 10, 11, 32-bit & 64-bit), macOS, and CUPS Linux are downloaded directly from the official Citizen photo site: `https://www.citizen.photo/` or `https://citizen-systems.com/en/products/printer/photo/cx-02`.
  - Kepler Tech does not host third-party driver binaries; customers should download the latest verified drivers from the official Citizen portal.

## 5. Ribbon Installation & Handling
- **Installation Steps**:
  - Open the front cover by pulling the release lever.
  - Load the yellow ribbon supply spool into the supply side and the clear take-up spool into the take-up side.
  - Rotate the take-up spool forward to eliminate any ribbon slack before closing the cover.
  - Ribbon rewind feature automatically saves unused ribbon sections for multi-format efficiency.

## 6. Accidental Damage & Warranty Coverage
- **Dropping / Impact Damage**:
  - Accidental drops, liquid spills, and external physical trauma are not covered under standard manufacturer warranty terms.
  - Hand off customer to the Kepler Tech service desk in Dubai for diagnostic inspection and repair quotes.
