## 2. Wireless Printing & Mobile Connectivity (AirCastPro)
- **AirCastPro Wireless Print Bridge**:
  - The Citizen CX-02 features a direct USB 2.0 interface. It does not have built-in Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.
  - For wireless printing from Apple iOS devices (iPhones, iPads), Android smartphones, and tablets, Kepler Tech provides the **AirCastPro 3 Wireless Print Server**.
  - AirCastPro connects directly to the CX-02 via USB and creates an instant Apple AirPrint and wireless hotspot network for instant wireless printing at events and photo booths.

## 6. Print Size Capabilities: Passports & Big Posters
- **Citizen CX-02 Size Limits**:
  - The Citizen CX-02 supports print formats up to 6x9 inches (specifically: 4x6", 5x7", 6x8", 6x9", and 2x6" photostrips).
- **Passport Photos**:
  - The CX-02 can print 2x2 inch and 35x45 mm ID/passport photos when formatted via photo booth or passport layout software on 4x6" media sets. Dedicated biometric passport capture kiosks are referred to technical sales.
- **Big Posters & Large Prints**:
  - The CX-02 cannot print large posters (e.g. A2, A1, A0, 24" or 44" wide). For big posters, Kepler Tech directs customers to our Epson SureColor Large Format category.
