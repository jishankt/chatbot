## Citizen Photo Printers Lineup and Comparison Matrix
Kepler Tech LLC (Dubai) is an authorized partner and distributor for Citizen Systems commercial dye-sublimation photo printers across the UAE, GCC, and Africa. The official Citizen photo printer lineup consists of four specialized commercial models:
1. Citizen CX-02: Flagship compact, high-speed 6-inch dye-sub printer for event photo party booths, wedding photographers, and retail kiosks. Supports 4x6, 5x7, 6x8, 5x5, 6x6, and 2x6 photo strips. Features ribbon rewind to eliminate media waste when printing odd 4x6 photos. Fast 8.4-second 4x6 print speed, 12 kg metal chassis.
2. Citizen CX-02W: Wide-format 8-inch dye-sublimation photo printer engineered for larger professional prints including 8x10 inch and 8x12 inch studio portraits, school packages, and panoramic multi-cuts (8x2 to 8x32 inches). Features driver grey calibration, ribbon rewind, and support for specialty Silver Pearl and Metallic photo media. 14 kg.
3. Citizen CY-02: High-capacity commercial dye-sublimation printer designed for demanding, unattended retail kiosks, busy photo shops, and theme park attractions. Holds up to 700 prints per roll on 4x6 inch (or 350 prints on 6x8 inch), minimizing downtime for roll refills. 13.8 kg.
4. Citizen CZ-01: Ultra-compact, highly portable 4-inch dye-sublimation photo printer weighing just 5.8 kg (occupies 31% volume of standard models). Ideal for on-the-go photographers and compact photo booths. Supports 4x6 and 4x4 square Instagram formats, features a built-in anti-curl mechanism for completely flat prints, and an advanced internal cooling system for continuous printing.

All four models feature a thermal dye-sublimation transfer process with clear protective overcoat (producing instantly dry, water-resistant, smudge-proof prints), high-speed USB 2.0 interface, compatibility with Windows 10/11 and macOS, and deliver crisp continuous-tone photo output at 300x300 dpi (High-Speed) and up to 300x600 dpi (High-Quality).

## Citizen Photo Printer Model Selection and Conflict Resolution Guide
When advising customers on selecting the right Citizen photo printer, the sales assistant evaluates specific technical workflow requirements:
1. Portability & Space Constraints:
   - If the customer prioritizes lightweight travel (<6 kg), tight photo booth kiosks, or mobile pop-up events: Recommend **Citizen CZ-01** (5.8 kg, 4x6 and 4x4 square, anti-curl mechanism).
2. High-Volume & Minimum Downtime:
   - If the customer runs busy theme parks, amusement attractions, unattended retail kiosks, or large festivals where roll reloading causes revenue loss: Recommend **Citizen CY-02** (700 prints per roll on 4x6, double the standard capacity).
3. Large Studio Portraits & Group Photos:
   - If the customer requires large prints (8x10, 8x12, A4) or luxury finishes (Silver Pearl / Metallic): Recommend **Citizen CX-02W** (8" wide format, grey calibration).
4. All-Around Event Booth Standard:
   - If the customer needs fast event printing, versatile 4x6, 5x7, 6x8 formats, ribbon rewind waste prevention, and durable metal construction: Recommend **Citizen CX-02** (8.4s 4x6 speed, 12 kg, carry bag available).

### Conflict Resolution for Customer Requirements:
- Conflict A (Large 8x10 prints vs 700-print roll capacity):
  If a customer asks for "8x10 prints with 700 prints per roll", explain the technical trade-off: The **Citizen CX-02W** is the only model that supports wide 8x10" and 8x12" prints (with 110-120 prints/roll). The high-capacity **Citizen CY-02** holds 700 prints per roll, but its maximum print width is 6 inches (supports 4x6" and 6x8"). Ask the customer whether large 8x10 format or roll capacity is their primary priority.
- Conflict B (Ultra-lightweight portable vs 8x10 large prints):
  If a customer asks for "ultra-lightweight under 6 kg for 8x10 prints", explain that the ultra-lightweight 5.8 kg model is the **Citizen CZ-01** (maximum 4-inch width, printing 4x6 and 4x4). For 8x10 inch wide-format output, the **Citizen CX-02W** weighs 14 kg. Ask whether portability or 8x10 print size is required.
- Conflict C (General or ambiguous recommendation without size/volume):
  If the customer asks "Which printer should I buy?" without specifying paper size or expected volume, ask two concise clarifying questions:
  1. What print sizes do you need to produce (e.g., standard 4x6", large 8x10", or compact 4x4" square)?
  2. What is your primary priority: maximum mobility (lightweight under 6 kg), highest volume per roll (up to 700 prints), or fast event photo booth printing?
