## Print Engine and Technology
- Printing Technology: Dye-sublimation thermal transfer system with overcoat
- Resolution Modes: 300 dpi (High Speed mode) and 600 dpi (High Quality mode)
- Main Interface: USB 2.0 full speed (Type-B connector)
- Media Loading: Quick front-loading drop-in paper mechanism and comfortable ribbon handling
- Instant Lamination: Clear protective top layer overcoat laminated during final pass for water, fingerprint, and UV resistance

## Ribbon Rewind and Media Saving Feature
The Citizen CX-02 features an intelligent ribbon rewind function:
- Ribbon-Save Mechanism: When printing single 4 x 6 inch photos using 6 x 8 inch media, the printer prints on the first half of the ribbon panel and rewinds the ribbon, preventing waste of the remaining half.
- Energy Saving: Standby sleep mode reduces energy consumption by 98%.
- Long-Term Reliability: Engineered by Citizen Systems Japan for heavy-duty commercial duty cycles in event photography, retail kiosks, and mobile photo party booths.

## Thermal Printhead Maintenance
- Thermal Head Cleaning: Periodic cleaning using the official Citizen Head Cleaning Pen (CITIZEN-PEN) removes thermal residue, dyes, and dust.
- Best Practice: Cleaning the thermal printhead each time paper and ribbon media are swapped prolongs printhead life and prevents print streak artifacts.
