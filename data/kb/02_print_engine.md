## Print Engine and Technology
- Printing Technology: Dye-sublimation thermal transfer with ribbon rewind technology
- Resolution Modes: 300 x 300 DPI (High Speed Mode) and 300 x 600 DPI (High Resolution Mode)
- Gradation: 256 levels per colour (8-bit per colour channel, total 16.7 million colours)
- Colour Ribbons: Yellow, Magenta, Cyan plus transparent protective overcoat
- Buffer Memory: 64 MB internal high-speed image buffer

## Ribbon Rewind and Ribbon-Save Feature
The Citizen CX-02 features an intelligent ribbon rewind function:
- Ribbon-Save Mechanism: When printing odd numbers of 4x6" photos on 6x8" media or half-format sizes, the printer rewinds unused ribbon sections.
- Cost Efficiency: Prevents wasted ribbon ink panels and maximizes total print yields per media roll.
- Zero Waste: Eliminates the need to print pairs of photos when only one is needed.
