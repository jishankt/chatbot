## Print Engine and Technology
- Printing Technology: Dye sublimation thermal system with overcoat
- Resolution: 300 dpi, 600 dpi
- Printing Modes: High Speed, High Quality
- Main Interface: USB 2.0 full speed
- Paper Load: Quick and easy media changing with drop-in paper loading and comfortable ribbon handling

## Ribbon Rewind and Media Saving Feature
The Citizen CX-02 features an intelligent ribbon rewind function:
- Media Saving: Ribbon rewind feature avoids wasted ribbon and paper when printing single 4 x 6 inch photos using 6 x 8 inch media.
- Energy Saving: Sleep mode reduces energy consumption by 98%.
- Longevity: Precision engineered for a long, hassle-free operating life.
