## Physical Dimensions and Weight
- Size (W x D x H): 275 mm x 366 mm x 170 mm (27.5 x 36.6 x 17 cm)
- Product Weight: 12 Kg (printer unit; 2kg lighter than the CX model and 15% smaller)
- Package Weight: 13.5 Kg
- Package Dimensions: 39 x 50 x 33.5 cm (50 x 39 x 33.5 cm)
- Portability & Footprint: Ultra-compact and lightweight chassis designed to fit into small, cramped spaces, photo booth enclosures, and retail kiosks, making it easy to travel, transport, and set up on location.

## Operating Systems and Connectivity
- Supported Operating Systems: Mac OS X, Windows 7 & 10, Windows 11, Windows Server 2022
- Main Interface: USB 2.0 full speed
- Energy Saving & Sleep Mode: Sleep mode reduces energy consumption by up to 98%, entering a low-power dormant state when inactive (automatically after a period of inactivity or manually initiated) while ensuring quick and seamless return to full printing functionality.
- Paper & Ribbon Handling: Quick and easy media changing with drop-in paper loading and comfortable ribbon handling.
- Printer Driver: Improved printer driver provides real-time media status monitoring and selectable finishing options (Glossy, Matte, Fine Matte, Luster).
