## Print Sizes and Capacity
- 4 x 6 inches (102 x 152 mm): 400 prints per roll, 800 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS46-2PC, estimated 800 prints per set)
- 5 x 7 inches (127 x 178 mm): 230 prints per roll, 460 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS57-2PC, verified 460 prints per set)
- 6 x 8 inches (152 x 203 mm): 200 prints per roll, 400 prints per 2-roll media box (SKU: CX2-MS68-2PC, estimated 400 prints per set)
- 6 x 9 inches (152 x 229 mm): 180 prints per roll (hardware supported; 6x9 media sets not stocked by Kepler Tech LLC)
- 2 x 6 inches (51 x 152 mm): Photo booth strips enabled through driver dual cut feature on 4x6" or 6x8" media

## Print Speeds
- 4 x 6 inch: 8.4 seconds (High Speed mode) / 9.8 seconds (High Quality mode)
- 5 x 7 inch: 14.2 seconds
- 6 x 8 inch: 15.6 seconds
- 6 x 9 inch: 20.8 seconds

## Finishes and Surface Control
- Finishes: Glossy, Matte, and Fine Matte (Luster) finishes available.
- Digital Control: Finishes are selected directly within the printer driver software using the thermal head temperature profile on the clear protective overcoat layer. No special paper or ribbon changes are required.
- Protective Lamination: Automatically applied as a 4th pass layer, providing water resistance, UV protection, and fingerprint resistance.

## Paper and Ribbon Loading
- Front-loading drop-in paper roll and ribbon cassette mechanism.
- Fast reloading: Designed for quick consumable replacement in under 60 seconds, even in compact photo booth enclosures or dark event environments.
- Removable scrap bin: Catches paper trims and edge cuts to maintain a clean workspace.
