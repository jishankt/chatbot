## Company Profile
Kepler Tech LLC is a premier technology distributor and provider of professional printing hardware, wide-format plotters, dye-sublimation photo systems, consumables, and digital imaging solutions based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Operating for over a decade (10+ years experience), Kepler Tech serves businesses, photo studios, event companies, print service providers, and resellers across the UAE, all six GCC countries, and the African continent.

## Core Specialization
Kepler Tech is an authorized distributor and dealer for world-leading printing brands including Epson and Citizen. The company provides complete end-to-end printing solutions encompassing:
1. Hardware supply: Office business printers, large format CAD/GIS plotters, graphic production printers, and compact dye-sublimation event photo printers.
2. Official consumables: OEM inks, pigment and dye cartridges, printheads, maintenance boxes, and specialized media sets.
3. Media & Fine Art: Inkjet photo papers, fine art rag, backlit films, and canvas from leading mills (Innova, Olmec, Korejet, Epson).
4. Technical Services: Certified maintenance, repair, printer rentals, leasing, and Managed Print Services (MPS).

## Facility and Team
Kepler Tech operates its main office and central distribution warehouse located on Khalid Bin Waleed Road in Dubai. The company maintains an in-house team of manufacturer-trained printer engineers and customer support specialists delivering pre-sales guidance, ICC profile color calibration, and remote/on-site after-sales technical support.
