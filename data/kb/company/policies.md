## Commercial Terms & Policies

### 1. Free Shipping Threshold & Delivery Fees
- Free Delivery: Orders over 400 AED qualify for free shipping within the UAE (per 2026 Countries We Serve policy; older 2023 terms state flat rate AED 10 for smaller deliveries; our team will confirm).
- Delivery Fees Outside UAE: Calculated at checkout based on destination country, freight weight, and carrier.

### 2. Order Processing & Transit Time
- Order Processing: All orders are processed within 24 hours (1 to 2 business days).
- UAE Domestic Transit: 1 to 3 business days (FAQ states 1 to 2 days; our team will confirm).
- GCC Regional Transit: 4 to 8 business days depending on destination country.
- International / Africa Transit: 6 to 8 business days via courier or customized freight schedule.

### 3. Payment Methods
- Accepted Payment Methods: Credit Card (Visa, MasterCard in AED), Debit Card, and PayPal. Payment is required at time of order placement.

### 4. Cancellation Policy
- Cancellation Window: Orders may be cancelled within 24 hours of placing the order prior to dispatch.

### 5. Return & Refund Policy
- Inspection & Packaging: Goods accepted for return only if defective or non-conforming, in original unopened delivery packaging.
- Defect Notification: Customer must notify Kepler Tech within 2 days of receipt.
- Refund Window: Refunds processed via original mode of payment within up to 45 days following inspection.

### 6. Warranty Terms
- Genuine Products: All hardware supplied by Kepler Tech carries full manufacturer warranty.
- Warranty Coverage: We provide genuine manufacturer-backed warranty support; specific duration and terms for individual models (including the Citizen CX-02 warranty) will be confirmed by our technical team.

### 7. VAT & Tax Treatment
- UAE VAT Treatment: UAE VAT status is not stated as fact without explicit configuration.
- Non-UAE GCC Countries: According to the official Countries We Serve specifications, prices for Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, and Oman include VAT.

### 8. Bulk Orders & Volume Discounts
- Volume Pricing: Volume discounts are available for commercial bulk orders, studio bundles, and reseller consignments.
- Quotation Process: Inquiries for bulk orders (or quantities exceeding 10 units) are routed directly to corporate sales specialists to compute tailored commercial rates.
