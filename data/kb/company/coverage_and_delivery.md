## 2. Pan-Africa Shipping
Kepler Tech ships commercial printing equipment, photo printers, large-format plotters, and media rolls across Africa through established international air and ocean cargo partners (Aramex, FedEx, and freight forwarders).
- Major Markets Served: Nigeria, South Africa, Kenya, Ghana, Ethiopia, Tanzania, Rwanda, Uganda, Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, Zambia, Mozambique, Namibia, Botswana, and more.
- Customs & Documentation: Kepler Tech assists with commercial invoices, certificate of origin, packing lists, and export declarations.
- Reseller & Bulk Shipments: Consolidated freight and customized volume quotations available via the corporate sales desk.
